## Narrow-use assessment

The proposed use is manual selection followed by 1–2 original, attributed observations. It is **not** an API collector, bulk scraper, review archive, copied passage, image reuse, model-training dataset or claimed consensus.

Public accessibility and absence of a license are different from an express restriction. Copyright does not protect facts or ideas, but does protect their expression. A short factual paraphrase has a different copyright risk from copying a review; attribution is not itself a permission grant. Fair use is case-specific, and whether website terms are binding/applicable to this narrow use is a separate question. These are issue flags, not a legal ruling that every original summary is unlawful. [Copyright Office facts/expression guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html) and [fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/).

These particular leads have **express terms**, not merely a missing Creative Commons license. The module therefore excludes their report content from customer-display data pending narrow-use clearance. A reviewed legal basis can clear a source without a new license; written permission is another route. A new source lacking a license is not automatically rejected for that reason alone.

## Proposed standalone contract

`src/data/reviewBriefPilot.ts` exposes a frozen edition and a synchronous optional selector. The caller supplies an already-resolved exact brand/product/variant/market. No fuzzy title matching, guessed barcodes or scanner imports. Off by default. Missing/withheld/mismatched brief returns `undefined`; the future UI omits the section without blocking the product result or changing Personal Fit.

Each future accepted observation needs original text, a visible qualification, source IDs and source-section locators. Each cited source needs an accepted exact report identity and a documented clearance basis. A ready brief has 1–2 observations. Conflicting experiences stay attributed/qualified rather than averaged; no counts, percentages, ratings, ingredient causes or personal outcomes are inferred.

Version 1.0.0 has **two withheld product records and zero displayable observations**. Both bloggers' reports remain editorial drafts only; the Reddit lead contributes none. This is a content/permission gap, not a completed customer pilot.

Integration decisions for Kanuj: confirm this contract; map existing exact identities; decide optional placement; resolve source clearance/market gaps. No shared schema is proposed. Brief data is not recommendation evidence and cannot be passed into the personal-fit engine. If later persistence attaches a brief, retain its exact version/snapshot separately; a new edition must not rewrite a saved assessment. The module itself has no save/update path.
