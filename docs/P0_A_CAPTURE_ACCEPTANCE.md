## Read-only QA findings at the capture checkpoint

1. `canCollectMore` is set for every processing failure. Terminal daily-limit/account-change screens cannot reach Add/Retake or retry through their outcome actions; remount initializes it true. Ordinary failure recovery sets it true again. No demonstrated new stale-reset bug in this state.
2. Before the follow-up fix, `processEvidence` checked React `session.phase`, not a synchronous gate. Two taps before rerender could run concurrent prepare/upload on one immutable target; the later call could receive an upload conflict even if the first succeeded. Root approved a bounded fix: `createCaptureOperationGate` acquires synchronously before callback side effects and releases in `finally`, including failure. E19 exercises the real gate with a deferred upload and actual processor; extra taps produce no extra grant preparation/upload. Bridge sequence checks remain intact.
3. Before the follow-up fix, `capturePhoto` waited for Camera while role chips remained interactive. Switching role before its promise returned could save the preview under the new role. Role selection now executes only when the same gate is idle, and role chips are disabled during the rendered camera-busy state. E19 defers camera completion and verifies role mutation/review cannot run until the original role's preview returns. Close and unmount invalidate gate tokens and request sequence; old completions cannot present results or unlock newer operations. Physical component behavior remains unverified.
4. An interrupted immutable upload with a lost success response is not proven recoverable by repeating upload. The helper intentionally uses `upsert: false`; there is no demonstrated immutable-object reconciliation API. Record as a platform recovery gap rather than changing Storage policy or claiming resume support.
