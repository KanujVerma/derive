### Payment options, without a selection

**Option A, free-first v1.0.** Ship a real hosted scanner-first Free Check with optional personalization and My Stuff after final engineering/device proof. Managed may be informational or invite-only, with no public activation or payment CTA in the released binary. Decide how any existing paid entitlement and account route is represented; do not imply that local fixture Managed screens are offered. This removes a public checkout dependency from v1.0, but still requires final source, hosted access, support and metadata parity.

**Option B, Managed payment included.** Specify exactly which managed benefits unlock in-app, which users see each CTA, the storefronts, and whether any entitlement or Apple in-app purchase path is required. The legacy `app/membership/index.tsx` launches a server-created Stripe Checkout URL through `Linking.openURL`; `src/services/remote/RemoteDeriveService.ts` invokes `create-membership-checkout`. Price is $25/month display, with server Price authoritative. A payment return does not grant membership until server truth confirms it. Hosted Checkout, signed webhook, Portal, cancellation and deletion/billing handling need live proof. Seek focused founder/legal/App Review analysis before activating billing. Apple's current guideline 3.1.1 says in-app feature unlocks use in-app purchase; 3.1.1(a) permits external purchase calls to action in U.S. storefront apps without an external-link entitlement, while rules differ elsewhere. Those provisions do **not** by themselves clear Derive's mixed digital/managed service model. [Apple App Review Guidelines, 3.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

## Account, permissions, claims and public policy

- **Deletion source:** `app/membership/index.tsx`, `app/profile/index.tsx` and `src/components/account/FreeAccountShell.tsx` surface confirmation and deletion. `src/services/accountDeletion.ts` invokes `delete-customer-account` and clears local state only after `{deleted:true}`. The Edge Function inventories and removes both private Storage namespaces, verifies emptiness, then calls `auth.admin.deleteUser`; relational cascades need final hosted proof. Retry/error text exists. Test a real final-candidate account, both storage buckets, relational rows, signed-out/free return and failure/retry. For any paid account, disclose what deletion does to Stripe subscription/billing and independently verify cancellation behavior. Apple requires in-app account deletion when creation is offered. [Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app) and [Guideline 5.1.1(v)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- **Camera and library:** `app.json` declares camera for skin progress/shelf and photo library for product uploads. `expo-camera` is used for Check/package and managed skin capture; `expo-image-picker` is used in other managed/chat paths. The camera string omits the scanner-first product barcode/package purpose, so final copy needs review with the actual binary. No microphone, location, contacts, tracking or Bluetooth purpose keys were found in `app.json`; inspect the aggregate native plist and prompts before release. Avoid unused permissions. [Guideline 5.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- **Cosmetic boundary:** `docs/SAFETY_PRIVACY.md` and site source say non-diagnostic cosmetic guidance; server safety logic directs pregnancy/prescription and urgent symptom questions to clinicians. Final in-app copy, screenshots, metadata and results must avoid diagnosis, cure, quantified smartphone measurements and unverified dermatologist/clinical review. Apple's medical guideline 1.4.1 applies extra scrutiny to potentially inaccurate health claims. [Apple App Review Guidelines, 1.4.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- **Age rating:** prepare questionnaire answers from the final binary's actual health/treatment references, user content, chat/AI, web links, ads and age assurance. Apple generates the regional rating from the questionnaire; do not assume “18+” equals an App Store rating or that a rating enforces an in-app adult audience. [Apple age rating reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions).
- **Public site source and live state:** `apple-site/` contains home, privacy, support and privacy choices. The privacy source says free Check is *in development* and hosted access is not enabled, matching the present beta but requiring an update if public Free Check ships. It also describes managed deletion routes. In-app `src/config/environment.ts` points to `https://derive-beta-site.vercel.app/{privacy,support,privacy-choices}`. The orchestrator's authorized live read on 2026-09-27 returned HTTP 200 for all four URLs and found the live home headline “Your skincare, handled.” while tracked `apple-site/index.html` says “Make sense of your skincare.” This confirms a live/source copy mismatch, not which version is release-correct. This worktree's direct fetch failed under sandbox DNS, so full live page bodies and deletion text were not independently compared here. Recheck exact public text, contact mailto, deletion instructions and final scanner/provider wording before ASC fields are set. Do not deploy from this audit.
- **Export compliance:** `app.json` declares `usesNonExemptEncryption=false`; source alone cannot establish the final dependency/crypto answer. Inspect archive and Apple export questionnaire before submission. Do not infer custom encryption or alter the declaration without evidence.
