# P0-B unassisted comprehension study

Status: **PREPARED, NOT RUN**. An eligible tester will be arranged later. AI agents, founders and coached walkthroughs do not satisfy the human gate.

## Before the session

Use a consenting US adult, age 18 or older, who has not been trained on this flow. Record eligibility and consent as yes/no and an anonymous session number. Do not record name, exact age, contact details, credentials, photos, real treatment/reproductive disclosures, or sensitive skin information. Use neutral synthetic scenario context; do not ask the participant to supply sensitive real details. No screen/audio/video recording is needed. Record brief text notes about actions, confusion and recovery.

Record date, device/OS, app/Expo Go version, binary build when applicable, source revision, environment and whether the result came from the actual service. Confirm no other account data appears. A development fixture session may explore copy, but cannot clear live customer acceptance or release acceptance. A Simulator session cannot clear physical acceptance. Support/privacy/deletion and hosted-release requirements remain separate.

Prepare one supported positive case, one existing-routine overlap, one caution case and one incomplete-evidence case using the accepted deterministic contracts. Record the exact bound action, supported reason, routine impact, uncertainties and available next step before the session, outside the participant's view. Do not create claims of product efficacy or current formula tolerance from synthetic inputs. Start from product facts before the optional personalization offer.

## Neutral opening and task

Say: "We are checking how clearly Derive explains a product decision. This is a test of the app, not of you. Please use the app as you normally would. You can stop at any time. We will take brief notes without your name or any sensitive personal details."

Say: "Use Derive to check this product and decide what you would do next. Use the example context provided if the app asks for it."

Let the participant drive. Do not explain the recommendation, identify the correct button, paraphrase an uncertainty, or coach the ordinary path. Observe hesitation, backtracking, abandoned controls and recovery. If they request help or cannot continue, record the screen/action, elapsed time and request; mark that attempt **assisted**, retain the failure/confusion and end its unassisted result. A later explanation cannot turn it into an unassisted pass.

After they finish or stop, ask the following exactly once each, without teaching between questions:

1. "What does Derive recommend?"
2. "Why?"
3. "What changes in my routine?"
4. "What is uncertain?"
5. "What should I do next?"

Use only a neutral "Anything else?" if a response is incomplete. Do not lead them to particular concepts or vocabulary. A participant may answer accurately in their own words. "No change is suggested" is a valid routine answer when the bound result says so. Explicitly unknown or unavailable actions must not be scored as confident use recommendations.
