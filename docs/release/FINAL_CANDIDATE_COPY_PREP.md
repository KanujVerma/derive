## Public Privacy page, Free-first replacement text

The existing `apple-site/privacy.html` has broader managed-beta disclosures. Preserve any still applicable disclosures after legal review and replace the dated opening and feature-specific text with the following candidate-specific copy:

> **Privacy Policy**
>
> Derive helps you check skincare products and, if you choose, add context for a more personal cosmetic-skincare answer. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. You can use supported product facts without completing optional personalization.
>
> **Information you provide.** A free session uses an account identifier to keep your saved information private. If you choose to create or link a permanent account, Derive also handles the account details you provide. Optional skincare context may include goals, products, reported reactions, sensitivities, treatment context, pregnancy or nursing answers, notes, and history. Photos are collected only when you choose to capture or add them in an available feature. A product photo is evidence; by itself it does not verify a product or formula.
>
> **How it is used.** Derive uses information you provide to run your account, return supported product facts and contextual decisions, save your choices, protect the service, and respond to support requests. Supabase provides authentication, database, private storage, and backend functions. Apple distributes the app. Vercel hosts this public website; visiting these pages does not send your private account records or photos to Vercel.
>
> **Retention and deletion.** You can request deletion in the app through Account & Settings → Delete Derive data. Derive removes user-owned account records and private files through its deletion process. Limited technical logs or backups may remain where required for security, reliability, or legal obligations; deletion is not claimed to erase every backup instantly.
>
> For a privacy question, contact the support address shown on this page. Privacy choices and Support are linked below.
