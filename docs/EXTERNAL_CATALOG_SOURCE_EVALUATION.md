# External barcode-source evaluation and licensing gate

Status: evaluation plan and source-terms review, 2026-09-28. This document does **not** select a production provider, authorize a bulk import, claim measured coverage, or change the canonical product/formula trust rules. Pricing and terms must be reconfirmed before a contract or release. This is an engineering gate, not legal advice.

## Decision to make

Run a parallel, broad-coverage **candidate-identity** workstream alongside manufacturer/package-backed canonical review. Optimize for the share of target-cohort Check attempts that produce a *useful, honestly labeled outcome*, not the count of manually ingested catalog rows. A decoder such as Expo Camera or Scandit yields a barcode number; it does not supply a product database. External lookup may yield a possible product name/brand/package, never a verified formula or personal verdict.

The existing S6 / `ProductTruthSnapshotV1` boundary remains authoritative. Keep external facts isolated from `products`, `product_variants`, `product_identifiers` with `verified_at`, and `product_formula_versions` until an independently supported, auditable promotion. An external ingredient string is not an exact package/formula version.

## Representative U.S. skincare barcode benchmark

No coverage percentage has been measured in this document. Freeze a **target-cohort** corpus before querying providers:

1. Collect scan **encounters** from multiple intended first users and shopping channels, using physical U.S.-retail skincare packages they own or would actually scan. Start with 20–30 encounters for a labeled early signal, then at least ~100 encounters before a launch-rate decision. Repeated popular GTINs are separate encounters in this **primary encounter-weighted denominator**; do not deduplicate them away or artificially balance the cohort. Report a separate **distinct-GTIN breadth/coverage slice**. Record category/channel, sampling method and repeat frequency; include cleansers, moisturizers, sunscreens, acne treatments, serums and lip/barrier care as encountered, and disclose missing slices. Independently observe each physical package's front-label name, brand, size/variant, GTIN digits/type, date and evidence/provenance, even when its GTIN repeats. Keep any rights-cleared package photos private and separate. Align the frozen corpus with the offline benchmark in [PR #162](https://github.com/KanujVerma/derive/pull/162).
2. Normalize UPC-A/EAN-13 aliases and validate check digits exactly once. Keep the original symbology and the normalized comparison key. Do not infer variant or formula from a shared GTIN.
3. Query OBF with an identifying User-Agent, a fixed single-thread budget below the published product-read limit, retry/backoff on 429/503, and no search-as-you-type. GS1 public lookup is manual/internal only and capped at 30/day; a larger GS1 test needs a contract. **Do not script Barcode Lookup's free test account**: its terms forbid automated access. An automated corpus run requires an authorized paid API plan or written vendor permission; count chargeable successful responses and record spend. Send only GTINs, not private user photos/context, and never place a provider key in the mobile bundle or a benchmark artifact.
4. Grade every source/GTIN against the independently observed package: `exact_package_identity`, `product_family_only`, `ambiguous_or_conflicting`, `wrong_product`, `not_found`, and `provider_error`. Separately score field completeness, freshness/observed date, U.S. relevance, response latency, API failure rate, rights to display/cache, and price per useful hit. A returned record is not automatically a useful hit.
5. Publish aggregate counts and Wilson confidence intervals with the encounter-weighted cohort mix and date. Report per-category/channel failure and wrong-match rates, union coverage, overlap, repeat-GTIN frequency, distinct-GTIN breadth/coverage, and **incremental lift over current canonical/catalog path**. Do not extrapolate from a convenience sample to all U.S. skincare. A partial source run is not a headline coverage rate.

The launch metric aligns with PR #162's `rates.usefulScanHit`: **physically decoded barcode + independently adjudicated exact or possible candidate + correctly qualified next action**, divided by **all eligible target-cohort scan encounters**, including repeat GTINs. A possible candidate must be presented as possible and offered for confirmation; a generic unknown fallback alone is not a useful scan hit. Also track wrong-product/false-certainty rate, distinct-GTIN breadth, user abandonment after unknown, source p95 latency, provider spend per useful hit and manual-review load. Exact-package identity and verified formula are separate, narrower metrics; a candidate never proves either canonical formula or personal fit.

## Read-through design gate

1. Local verified canonical match takes precedence. A valid unrecognized GTIN can call a **server-side** external candidate adapter behind an explicit provider/rights allowlist, timeout, quota, circuit breaker and per-source feature flag. No API keys in client code; no phone-to-provider call unless the license and privacy review explicitly allow it.
2. Return a bounded candidate record: source ID/type, GTIN observed, provider's brand/name/size, source record URL or ID, retrieved/observed timestamps, rights policy version, and exact fields shown. Treat conflicting providers as ambiguity. Never set `verified_at`, formula ID, `verified_product_formula`, or a product-truth snapshot from the adapter response.
3. The customer may confirm a candidate *label* and provide package evidence; that does not promote its chemistry. S6 case/evidence and review queue keep source provenance and conflict. If rights prohibit persistent storage, retain only permitted operational metadata and fetch anew for display; do not smuggle provider data into a canonical row through user confirmation.
4. Formula display and personal decision continue only from independently verified, version-bound truth. The no-match path must still give useful recovery (search, package evidence, or review) without a fabricated identification.
5. Before activation, prove source-specific deletion/TTL, user-facing attribution, privacy disclosures, license-compatible caching, monitoring of source errors, and a kill switch. A GS1 public lookup adapter is explicitly disabled for customer traffic.

## Questions that must be answered before production

- **Open Beauty Facts / legal:** Does the proposed ephemeral read-through plus any retained candidate metadata create an adapted/derivative database under ODbL? What attribution and share-alike notices/exports would be required? Can we keep OBF data logically separate from proprietary canonical truth, and what are the obligations if later reviewed into it? What are OBF's current cosmetics-instance rate limits and U.S. coverage? Ask OFF about the specific API usage and high-volume plan.
- **GS1 US:** What commercial API/batch product-identity product is available to a non-GS1-member consumer app? May Derive display results to consumers and retain/cache them? For how long, at what query volume and cost? Are brand-owner images usable? How are corrections and stale data handled? Does the healthcare-products limitation in the public ToU affect any sunscreen/acne-treatment SKU in the intended use case?
- **Barcode Lookup:** Does the subscription permit customer-facing display of product titles and facts in a paid/free app? Which returned fields can be cached, for how long, and are derivative records allowed? How must deletion be proven across backups after termination? What rights to descriptions/images are separately needed? Will the vendor explicitly authorize a scripted benchmark on a paid account or an exception to the free-test restriction? What are rate/overage behavior, uptime/support and DPA terms? Never transmit private photos or context.
- **Scandit:** Is the student's/community key usable for a commercial Derive app? What is the smallest production license, minimum commitment, device/scan definition, overages and SDK expiry behavior? Can its analytics be disabled, and what exact telemetry (including decoded barcode or pixels) leaves the device under each setting? Does a native Expo development/TestFlight build satisfy the licensed app-ID configuration?

## Immediate 24-hour sequence

1. Freeze the private corpus and baseline existing Check outcomes; publish the denominator and stratification before seeing provider results.
2. Run OBF candidate-only reads inside a rate budget; run at most 30 GS1 public manual/internal comparisons in 24 hours; run no automated Barcode Lookup queries on its free test account. A scripted commercial benchmark requires an authorized paid plan or written permission. Record misses, misidentifications, latency and terms status, not just hits.
3. Implement a source-neutral candidate adapter and contract tests with fixtures, **disabled in production** until rights are resolved. Keep the canonical ingestion path separate and available for reviewed manufacturer/package facts.
4. Decide production source(s) only from incremental useful-hit lift **and** written rights/cost/privacy acceptance. A strong OBF hit rate alone does not clear ODbL obligations; a strong GS1 result does not override the public ToU.
