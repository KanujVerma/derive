Privacy choices

You can delete your Shelv account in the app. You do not need to email support first.

Before beta access is active: open Founding Beta Access and choose Delete Account.

After managed access is active: open Account and choose Delete Account.

In a free Check session, where available: open Account & Settings and choose Delete Shelv data.

Questions about privacy can also go to kanuj.verma12@gmail.com. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive medical information in email.
