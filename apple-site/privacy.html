Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Founding Beta of Derive, a cosmetic skincare management app. Derive does not diagnose medical conditions and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Information you provide

  • Account information: first name, last name, and email address, plus the password you choose. Derive does not store that password itself; Supabase Auth handles it.
  • Skincare context you enter: goals, preferences, current products, reactions, sensitivities, prescription context, pregnancy or nursing disclosure, notes, check-ins, and routine history.
  • Photos you choose to add: baseline face or skin photos, product or shelf photos, and check-in photos when that capture is available.

Information used to operate the account

Derive keeps the account identifier and session needed to sign you in, keep your records private to you, and secure the service. This external beta does not send your information to an advertising network.

How information is used

Derive uses this information to provide the beta, maintain your account and access, store onboarding context, prepare and show your routine and progress, protect the account, and respond to support requests. Direct feedback may be used to improve reliability. AI-enabled Ask and Scan are not part of this external beta build. If Derive later sends personal data to a model provider, this policy will be updated before that use is relied on for beta customers.

Who processes it

  • Supabase provides authentication, the database, private file storage, and backend functions for the Derive account. Customer photos and account records are stored there for the beta.
  • Apple and TestFlight distribute the beta app and may receive the beta feedback you submit through Apple.
  • Vercel hosts this public website. Visiting these pages does not send your skin photos or account records to Vercel.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while needed to provide your account and for legitimate security and operational needs. You can permanently delete your account inside Derive from Founding Beta Access before activation, or from Account after access is active. That deletion removes your user-owned private files and account records through Derive's deletion process. Limited technical logs or backups may remain for a short time where needed for security, reliability, or legal obligations. Deletion is not claimed to erase every backup instantly.
